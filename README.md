- **7-Slide Motion Graphic Presentation**: Cover, Overview, Joint Collaboration, PSIS PP, AYLA BTB, Boosting & Ads, Evidence Gallery.
- **Horizontal Swipe & Touch Navigation**: Smooth drag, arrow keys, bottom dock indicators, floating controls.
- **Full Creative Work Management**: 86 Tasks, 86 Deliverables, 87 Verified Evidence Items, 15 Boosting Campaigns.
- **Offline & Cross-Device Compatible**: Zero external dependencies; pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.
